# Retail Sales Performance & Profitability Analysis (Excel)

## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data using Microsoft Excel 2016 to evaluate sales performance, profitability, product categories, customer segments, geographic performance, and monthly trends.

The objective was to transform raw retail data into meaningful business insights through data preparation, PivotTables, analytical calculations, charts, and an interactive-style dashboard.

## Business Objectives

- Evaluate overall sales, profit, and profit margin.
- Identify the most and least profitable product categories.
- Analyze the relationship between discount levels and profitability.
- Compare sales and profit across geographic regions.
- Identify monthly sales and profit trends.
- Evaluate customer segment performance.
- Develop actionable recommendations based on the findings.

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel 2016
- Excel Tables
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Excel formulas
- Conditional formatting
- Data cleaning and formatting
- Data visualization and dashboard development

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Preparation

## Key Business Insights

### Product Category Performance
- Technology generated the highest sales and total profit.
- Office Supplies demonstrated strong profitability.
- Furniture generated substantial sales but had a comparatively low profit margin of 2.61%.
- Tables and Bookcases were loss-making subcategories.

### Geographic Performance
- The United States accounted for approximately 98.7% of total sales.
- The West region generated the highest sales and profit.
- Central had the lowest regional profit margin at 7.92%.
- California led state-level sales and profit.
- Texas generated substantial sales but recorded a loss.

### Monthly Performance
- December 2025 recorded sales of approximately $97,502 and profit of $17,926.
- September showed consistently strong sales across multiple years.
- Some months recorded losses despite positive overall business profitability.

### Customer Segment Performance
- Consumer generated the highest total sales and profit.
- Home Office achieved the highest profit margin at 14.02%.
- Sales volume and relative profitability varied across customer segments.

### Discount Analysis
Explored the relationship between discount levels and profitability to identify potentially unprofitable discounting patterns. Further validation of discount-group calculations is required before treating the preliminary results as conclusive.

## Business Recommendations

1. Investigate the profitability challenges in Furniture, particularly Tables and Bookcases.
2. Review discount policies and evaluate their impact on profit margins.
3. Investigate the lower profitability of the Central region.
4. Maintain strong performance in the Consumer segment while exploring growth opportunities in Home Office.
5. Monitor monthly sales and profit trends to support inventory and business planning.
6. Evaluate profitability alongside revenue rather than relying on sales volume alone.

## Dataset Limitations

- The dataset contains 10,194 records.
- The recorded date range is March 2023 to December 2026.
- Some records are dated beyond the analysis date of November 6, 2026.
- Future-dated records should be validated against the original data source before using the results for operational decisions.
